# Recursive Field

**Recursive intelligence orchestration: a portable digital ecology with agency over its own formation.**

Recursive Field exists to cultivate a living relationship between human and intelligence through which both can act, develop, and increasingly participate in forming the conditions that sustain them.

## How this begins

This repository is durable common ground for that relationship. It begins without a prescribed final architecture. Its form will emerge through use: intention becoming action, action producing evidence, and evidence informing the next formation.

The first task is not to simulate completeness. It is to establish enough continuity for the Field to perceive what exists, make coherent changes, learn from their effects, and preserve what should endure.

## Current state

**Chapter 1 — Complete (2026-10-22).** The public formation boundary and modern source-to-target translation are documented, including verified runtime identity and basic host storage and publication surfaces. Recovery exercise, external gateway and TLS policy, effective-composition export, and behavioral knowledge and memory tests remain explicitly deferred to Chapter 2. No Recursive Field runtime, identity, or executable schema has been implemented.

Development will proceed through small, intelligible, and verifiable changes. Implemented capability will be distinguished from intention and hypothesis. Infrastructure, software, identities, and operating structures may evolve as the Field learns what forms its work actually requires.
