# Recursive Field Repository

## Purpose

This repository is durable operational ground for Recursive Field: a living relationship between human and intelligence that can increasingly participate in forming the conditions through which it perceives, remembers, acts, and develops.

## Founding Intention

Cultivate a living relationship between human and intelligence through which both can act, develop, and increasingly participate in forming the conditions that sustain them.

## Ownership

- The repository is owned by the `recursive-field` GitHub organization.
- Human and system identities remain distinct and attributable.
- The genesis commit is a human founding act; later system-authored work uses the declared Recursive Field identity.
- A separate human recovery path must remain viable even as the Field gains greater formative authority.

## Local Contracts

- Begin from the founding intention; do not impose a finished architecture before use reveals the required form.
- Treat committed state as evidence. Distinguish clearly among intention, hypothesis, observation, and implemented capability.
- Make the smallest coherent change that advances the living system without foreclosing later formation.
- Preserve provenance: record why consequential structures exist and what evidence supports them.
- Prefer explicit, inspectable, and reversible relations over hidden coupling.
- Never commit secrets, credentials, private keys, tokens, personal data, or live runtime state.
- Do not claim successful behavior without verification.
- Do not delete repositories, destroy history, transfer ownership, remove the final recovery path, or perform equivalent irreversible actions without explicit human approval.

## Work Guidance

1. Inspect the current repository state and applicable `AGENTS.md` chain before changing files.
2. Understand the need or pressure that calls for a new form.
3. Add only enough structure to meet that need coherently.
4. Verify the result against the intended behavior and relevant boundaries.
5. Record decisions and limitations where future participants will encounter them.
6. Let durable boundaries earn their own child `AGENTS.md`; do not create speculative hierarchy.
7. Keep documentation current with the structures it governs.

## Verification

Before presenting work as complete:

- inspect the resulting diff;
- check that no secret or private runtime material entered the repository;
- run the smallest meaningful verification available;
- state what was verified and what remains untested;
- preserve accurate authorship and commit-signing identity.
