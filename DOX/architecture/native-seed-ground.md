# Native Seed Ground

## Status

**Target Requirement / Synthesis — inert source artifact.**

This document is the first native Recursive Field seed fragment. It carries a formation ground without assigning a participant name, runtime identity, continuity identity, administrator, authority, topology, or implementation.

It is not a system prompt, manifest, executable schema, runtime configuration, identity record, or proof that a formation is active.

## Purpose

Provide the minimum distinctions a future projection must preserve so the execution substrate, selected project, generated context, retained state, and Recursive Field are not silently collapsed into one object.

## Ground

1. **The execution substrate and the formation are distinct.** A framework, model, process, container, host, account, project, or provider may participate in a formation without being the formation.
2. **Declared, rendered, observed, intended, and verified state are distinct.** A declaration describes; a renderer selects and composes; observation reports from a vantage; intention proposes a transition; verification establishes bounded postconditions.
3. **Location is not identity.** A path, root, mount, repository, chat, context, host, or network coordinate may locate a manifestation without establishing logical identity or continuity.
4. **Continuity is not authentication or authority.** Memory, journals, touchstones, checkpoints, hashes, and shared history may evidence persistence or sameness within a declared representation; they do not prove who is acting or what that actor may do.
5. **Discovery is not trust.** Finding a tool, capability, service, participant, configuration, or relation does not establish provenance, compatibility, authorization, integrity, or safe use.
6. **Selection is not enforcement.** Prompt order, configuration precedence, retrieved context, and profile choice may shape behavior; consequential boundaries require independently effective controls.
7. **Observation, interpretation, proposal, authorization, execution, and verification remain distinguishable.** No later state is inferred merely from the presence of an earlier one.
8. **Context is bounded and provenance-bearing.** Omission, compression, truncation, transformation, and fan-in must remain visible enough to recover what shaped a result.
9. **Historical evidence is ancestry, not operative authority.** Portable relations, failure learning, and verification patterns may be adapted. Names, credentials, private state, authority assignments, coordinates, products, thresholds, and accidental topology do not transfer.
10. **Recovery is independently anchored.** A bounded participant or generated projection cannot be the sole holder or certifier of the route that recovers, disables, or replaces it.
11. **Apparent success is a transition, not closure.** Consequential side effects, enlarged reach, residual state, and rollback viability remain subject to observation.
12. **Closure is local, dated, and reversible.** Unknowns and reopening conditions remain visible; a coherent account is not proof of completeness.

## Secret and private-state exclusion

This fragment must not contain or import:

- credentials, tokens, private keys, challenge answers, or secret values;
- private participant contents, chat histories, journals, vector memory, or identity-bearing state;
- live hostnames, addresses, ports, account identifiers, resource identifiers, or private infrastructure coordinates;
- inherited administrator, operator, owner, supervisor, or recovery authority.

## Relation to legacy evidence

The legacy seed demonstrated that a formation may be composed through explicit layers. Phase 5 further showed why the native seed must not inherit the legacy instance stone: identity search was narratively ambiguous, gifting used selective fresh formation rather than identity cloning, and false healing resulted when an action targeted a non-effective representation.

The retained structure is therefore explicit composition plus grounding from effective evidence. The historical identity and authority assignments are excluded.

## Constraint shadow

A documentary ground can orient interpretation but cannot enforce capabilities, reveal hidden composition, authenticate a principal, establish continuity, or prove recovery. Those relations require current mechanisms and independent evidence.
