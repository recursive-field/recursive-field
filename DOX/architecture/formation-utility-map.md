# Formation Utility Map

## Purpose

Recover the neutral utility of a formation-support harness against the current modern substrate without naming private origins, copying historical mechanisms, or selecting implementation. The map asks what each capability must make observable before a dependency-ordered build pathway can be trusted.

## Reading the Map

Consequential claims use **Verified Current**, **Target Requirement**, **Decision**, **Open**, and **Not Implemented**. The separate disposition vocabulary describes treatment of the current modern relation, not implementation status:

- **Native** — a **Verified Current** substrate relation appears reusable without changing its basic role.
- **Adapt** — a current relation supplies part of the utility but needs a formation-level boundary, contract, or observation surface.
- **New** — the target utility has no implemented Recursive Field counterpart.
- **Open** — available evidence is insufficient to choose among materially different dispositions.
- **Retire** — a historical mechanism or accident is excluded from the parity target; its underlying utility may survive elsewhere.

Observable parity means evidence of equivalent utility, not reproduction of historical structure.

## Thirteen Capabilities

### 1. Replaceable runtime boundary

- **Intention:** Permit a runtime manifestation to be replaced without losing private formation continuity or participant-scoped durable state.
- **Operative relation:** Authoritative source and durable inputs -> replaceable runtime manifestation -> observable service and generated projections.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Adapt.** Source, writable runtime, and user-owned persistent state are distinguishable. The continuity-state observation narrows required continuity to a relational closure spanning authoritative inputs, participant records, required capabilities, secret custody, and recovery material; restoration and cross-manifestation continuity remain **Open**.
- **Dependencies:** State-region/capability boundary; versioned source/projection provenance; participant continuity linkage; graduated operational authority.
- **Observable parity condition:** A fresh manifestation can be related to the same declared continuity, reconstruct intended projections, and show that runtime-local loss does not erase authoritative state.
- **Discriminating question:** Which members of the relational closure are actually required by one bounded context, and what replacement evidence distinguishes semantic continuity from surviving bytes?

### 2. Scoped composition and precedence

- **Intention:** Resolve situated inputs and capabilities by explicit scope and deterministic precedence rather than hidden coupling.
- **Operative relation:** Declared scopes + ordered candidate sources -> winning input for a bounded context.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Native.** **Verified Current** project, profile, user, plugin, and core resolution already provides ordered precedence where documented.
- **Dependencies:** Public-safe scope classes; state-region/capability boundary; versioned provenance.
- **Observable parity condition:** For a bounded request, the winning source and shadowed alternatives can be explained without exposing private coordinates.
- **Discriminating question:** Where does current precedence select an input, and where is additional policy enforcement required?

### 3. State-region/capability boundary

- **Intention:** Keep participant-scoped durable state, shared resources, policy/capability material, generated artifacts, and secrets distinguishable in ownership and lifecycle.
- **Operative relation:** State or capability class -> declared owner and mutability -> permitted consumers and persistence behavior.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Adapt.** Current state classes, mutation paths, persistence carriers, and recovery surfaces are differentiated by the continuity-state observation. Formation-wide ownership, trust, enforcement, and lifecycle boundaries remain **Open**.
- **Dependencies:** Seven-plane boundary; scoped composition; graduated operational authority.
- **Observable parity condition:** Each consequential input can be classified by owner, scope, mutability, durability, authorization, and rebuild semantics.
- **Discriminating question:** For one bounded context, which durable and shared state classes are selected dependencies, and which boundaries actually constrain access, mutation, execution, or recovery?

### 4. Versioned source/projection provenance

- **Intention:** Explain which versioned authoritative inputs produced a generated projection without making the projection authoritative.
- **Operative relation:** Attributable source and continuity inputs + assembly rules -> projection evidence -> reversible provenance relation.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Adapt.** Public source revision and subordinate generated projections are distinguished. Knowledge-source import retains private provenance fragments, but complete context, memory-composition, and capability implementation/configuration provenance is **Not Implemented**.
- **Dependencies:** Scoped composition; state-region/capability boundary; explicit evidence classes.
- **Observable parity condition:** An observer can trace a bounded projection to versioned or attributable inputs, identify uncertainty, and rebuild or invalidate it deliberately.
- **Discriminating question:** What minimum provenance survives projection rebuild while keeping private continuity and secrets outside public lineage?

### 5. Participant continuity linkage

- **Intention:** Preserve a participant's situated continuity across runtime replacement without equating a chat, project adapter, path, or manifestation with formation identity.
- **Operative relation:** Participant-scoped durable state <-> private formation continuity <-> one or more replaceable manifestations.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Open.** A persisted chat can resolve a mutable project name and derived project roots, while memory, chats, scheduler intent, settings, capabilities, and secrets can participate in continuity across different scopes. Durable formation identity, relocation-stable reverse resolution, and portable continuity linkage remain unresolved.
- **Dependencies:** Replaceable runtime boundary; state ownership; provenance; recovery authority; inheritance rules.
- **Observable parity condition:** Authorized evidence can follow continuity into a replacement manifestation and back again without disclosing private coordinates or cloning authority.
- **Discriminating question:** What relation proves continuing participation when adapter title, path, chat, host, or runtime identifier changes?

### 6. Context assembly with separated concerns

- **Intention:** Assemble model context from contextual initialization inputs while preserving the distinctions among instruction, knowledge, memory, policy, capability, and generated content.
- **Operative relation:** Scoped inputs + precedence + bounded assembly rules -> generated model context.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Adapt.** Instruction and prompt assembly are **Verified Current**. Direct inputs, conditionally retrieved knowledge and memory, settings, capabilities, and policy remain incompletely separated in observable output; behavioral memory evidence and formation-level provenance remain **Open** or **Not Implemented**.
- **Dependencies:** Scoped composition; state-region/capability boundary; versioned provenance; memory behavior.
- **Observable parity condition:** A bounded context identifies contributing concern classes, winning inputs, omissions, and projection effects without exposing their private contents.
- **Discriminating question:** Which concerns are assembled directly, retrieved conditionally, or applied as policy outside the rendered context?

### 7. Runtime policy/mode selection

- **Intention:** Select operating policy and mode for a bounded context while distinguishing preference, selection, authorization, and enforcement.
- **Operative relation:** Situated scope + declared policy inputs + authority -> effective runtime mode and constraints.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Open.** Profiles, settings, extensions, and tool lifecycle surfaces are **Verified Current**, but their formation-level policy relation and enforcement authority are unresolved.
- **Dependencies:** Scoped composition; capability boundary; context assembly; graduated operational authority.
- **Observable parity condition:** The selected mode, policy sources, enforcing boundaries, and fallback behavior are inspectable for one execution context.
- **Discriminating question:** Which current selectors alter composition only, and which can reliably prohibit or constrain action?

### 8. Effective formation inspection

- **Intention:** Explain the effective formation for a bounded context, including applicable inputs, winning precedence, capabilities, policies, and unresolved authority.
- **Operative relation:** Authoritative inputs + runtime resolution evidence -> public-safe effective formation inspection.
- **Current modern disposition:** **New.** A context-specific formation-level export is **Not Implemented**.
- **Dependencies:** Provenance; scoped composition; context assembly; policy/mode selection; capability trust.
- **Observable parity condition:** An inspection can account for consequential composition and its evidence without becoming a manifest, canonical root, desired state, or authority source.
- **Discriminating question:** Can one content-free report identify the state-class dependencies and winning roots required by a bounded context, including the exact points where resolution falls back to mutable coordinates or global stores?

### 9. Validated formation lifecycle

- **Intention:** Make formation creation, activation, change, replacement, suspension, and retirement observable, reversible, and evidence-bearing.
- **Operative relation:** Authorized lifecycle intent -> validated transition -> effect evidence -> recovery or rollback route.
- **Current modern disposition:** **New.** No Recursive Field lifecycle is implemented; current startup and response-loop behavior are evidence about the substrate only.
- **Dependencies:** Replaceable runtime boundary; participant continuity linkage; effective inspection; graduated authority; readiness criteria.
- **Observable parity condition:** Each supported transition checks prerequisites, records attributable effects, rejects invalid state, and preserves a tested recovery route.
- **Discriminating question:** What minimum transition set is necessary before a manifestation can be called formation-managed rather than merely running?

### 10. Portable recovery path

- **Intention:** Reconstitute continuity and service from declared source and durable state without undocumented coordinates or a single runtime's survival.
- **Operative relation:** Independent recovery authority + attributable source + recoverable durable state -> validated reconstitution on a replacement substrate.
- **Current modern disposition:** **New.** Public Git history, Agent Zero backup, Time Travel history, secret custody, and host/provider snapshots are distinct recovery surfaces with materially different coverage. Restoration, archive integrity, retention, compatibility, and independent recovery remain **Open**.
- **Dependencies:** Runtime boundary; state ownership; provenance; continuity linkage; validated lifecycle; recovery authority.
- **Observable parity condition:** An authorized exercise reconstructs the intended inhabitant on a replacement substrate, verifies integrity and continuity, and records unresolved losses.
- **Discriminating question:** Which minimum source, state, custody, and verification set is sufficient for reconstitution without restoring runtime accidents?

### 11. Health/audit/verification evidence

- **Intention:** Distinguish liveness, readiness, policy conformance, continuity integrity, and recovery evidence rather than collapsing them into one health signal.
- **Operative relation:** Declared claim -> bounded check -> attributable result -> status and reopening condition.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Adapt.** A local health response and documentary verification practices are **Verified Current**, but formation readiness and parity evidence are **Not Implemented**.
- **Dependencies:** Provenance; effective inspection; lifecycle states; recovery semantics; capability trust.
- **Observable parity condition:** Evidence identifies what was checked, from which vantage, against which expectation, with failures and untested claims remaining explicit.
- **Discriminating question:** Which checks demonstrate service liveness only, and which establish readiness, continuity, authorization, or recoverability?

### 12. Authorized ingress/service boundary

- **Intention:** Expose intended services through an explicit authorization, termination, routing, and observability boundary.
- **Operative relation:** External request -> authorized ingress boundary -> intended service -> attributable response and audit evidence.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Open.** Basic publication is **Verified Current**; external gateway, authorization, TLS, firewall, and tunnel intent remain unresolved.
- **Dependencies:** Runtime policy/mode selection; graduated authority; health/readiness evidence; service ownership.
- **Observable parity condition:** A request's admitted route, identity or authorization decision, termination point, target service, and denial behavior can be verified without treating reachability as security.
- **Discriminating question:** What is the intended external boundary, and which layer is responsible for admitting, terminating, routing, and recording access?

### 13. Graduated operational authority

- **Intention:** Increase operational agency through explicit, bounded tiers while retaining attribution, reversibility, and independent recovery authority.
- **Operative relation:** Actor + scope + capability trust + policy -> permitted observation, proposal, mutation, verification, or recovery action.
- **Current modern disposition:** **Open.** Independent human recovery authority is a **Decision** and self-formation agency is **Not Implemented**; delegation conditions remain unresolved.
- **Dependencies:** Provenance; state and capability ownership; policy enforcement; audit evidence; recovery semantics.
- **Observable parity condition:** Each consequential action is attributable to an authorized tier, constrained to its scope, independently verifiable, and recoverable when required.
- **Discriminating question:** Which evidence must be present before authority advances from observation to proposal, bounded actuation, wider operation, or recovery delegation?

## Explicit Retirement Set

**Decision:** The following historical mechanisms or operating accidents are retired from the parity target. This records exclusions from target utility; it does not assert that any named private system currently uses them.

| Retired disposition | Reason for retirement | Utility retained elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Implicit absolute coordinates as identity or discovery authority | Location is mutable and leaks deployment accident into continuity. | Public-safe scope resolution and portable continuity linkage. |
| A mutable runtime root as the sole durable record | Runtime replacement can erase or fork continuity. | Declared durable data and replaceable manifestations. |
| Duplicate writable aliases and broad writable roots | Ambiguous ownership prevents reliable mutation and recovery reasoning. | Explicit state regions, owners, and permitted mutation paths. |
| Generated projections treated as source or recovery authority | Derivations can be stale, incomplete, or rebuilt from different inputs. | Versioned provenance and explicit promotion decisions. |
| Undifferentiated configuration, secrets, state, and generated material | Mixed lifecycles increase leakage and make reconstitution non-discriminating. | Seven-plane separation and symbolic secret relations. |
| Hidden precedence or direct text mutation as lifecycle control | Effects cannot be reliably attributed, validated, or reversed. | Scoped composition and validated lifecycle transitions. |
| Mutable version labels or runtime-specific identifiers as continuity identity | Replacement or copying would conflate manifestation with continuity. | Attributable source versions and distinct continuity linkage. |
| Existence-only checks, local snapshots, or writable storage as recovery proof | Presence does not establish integrity, restoration, retention, or independent custody. | Authorized recovery exercise and acceptance-bearing evidence. |
| Reachability or direct publication as complete ingress policy | Connectivity does not establish authorization, termination, routing intent, or security. | Authorized ingress/service boundary. |
| Discoverability as capability trust or permission | Finding a capability does not establish compatibility, authorization, or safe lifecycle behavior. | Capability trust evidence and graduated authority. |
| Clone-and-patch reproduction as validated formation lifecycle | Unbounded copying obscures provenance, identity, state ownership, and rollback. | Declared composition, validation, reconstitution, and verification. |

## Present Map Boundary

This map recovers utility and discriminating questions only. It does not define identity, canonical roots, schemas, manifests, desired state, hard routing, code, configuration, deployment, or operational authority. **Target Requirement:** the eventual result of Chapter 2 study is a dependency-ordered, acceptance-bearing build pathway grounded in observable relations, not reproduction of historical mechanism.
