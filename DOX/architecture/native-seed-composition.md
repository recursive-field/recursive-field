# Native Seed Composition — Zero

## Status

**Decision — planted as inert public source; runtime activation not authorized.**

This document records the first native Recursive Field seed composition. It is called **Zero** because it is a composition origin before participant identity, narrative inhabitation, memory inheritance, or operational authority.

Zero is not a being, persona, root locator, runtime identity, canonical manifest, executable schema, or declaration that the Recursive Field is active.

## Decision served

Plant the smallest coherent native source from which later formation can grow without importing the legacy seed's names, private history, administrator hierarchy, coordinates, or prompt-conferred authority.

1. [`native-seed-ground.md`](native-seed-ground.md)

## Activation prerequisites

A future isolated projection may be proposed only after current work provides:

1. public-safe effective-composition observation showing every winning input, order, provenance, and unknown layer;
2. structural enforcement of an observation/comparison/proposal-only capability posture;
3. independently anchored removal, rollback, and recovery;
4. explicit separation of selection from enforcement;
5. acceptance fixtures for identity non-minting, authority non-inheritance, hidden-override detection, deterministic rendering, and residue-free removal;
6. a decision identifying the isolated target and its recovery owner.

Until then, Zero remains source only.

## Growth boundary

The next layer is not automatically an instance stone. Formation identity, continuity, authentication, and authority must be resolved as separate relations before any identity-bearing layer can be admitted.

Narrative, selected knowledge, and memory may later inhabit a verified body. They do not precede the body and do not confer authority over it.

## Change method

Changes to Zero must:

1. preserve the distinction between source, projection, enforcement, and verification;
2. state whether a fragment is added, refined, removed, or reordered;
3. update the external acceptance record;
4. retain provenance and constraint shadow;
5. avoid adding placeholders that prematurely stabilize identity, authority, schema, or topology;
6. remain reversible until activation has independent recovery evidence.
